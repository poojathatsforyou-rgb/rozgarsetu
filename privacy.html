1. Introduction

At RozgarSetu (accessible from our portal and mobile web apps), one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and how we use it in strict compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act and international privacy frameworks.

2. Google AdSense & Advertising Partners

Google is one of the third-party vendors on our site. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our portal and other sites on the internet. Users may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Our advertising partners include Google AdSense and Google Ad Manager. These third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons that are used in their respective advertisements and links that appear on RozgarSetu.

3. Log Files & Analytics

RozgarSetu follows standard procedures of using log files. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

4. Study Material & Non-Downloadable DRM

To protect educational copyright and intellectual property, study materials, mock test questions, and exam blueprints rendered on RozgarSetu are designed strictly for interactive online study. We do not sell user reading habits or academic performance logs to third-party data brokers.

5. Children's Information

Another part of our priority is adding protection for children while using the internet. RozgarSetu does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you think that your child provided this kind of information on our website, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately.

6. Grievance Officer & Contact

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact our compliance desk at support@rozgarsetu.in.
