Our Mission: Equal Opportunity Education & Job Alerts

RozgarSetu is India's premier free educational alert and examination preparation ecosystem. Founded with the mission to bridge the information divide between urban and rural aspirants, we aggregate verified notifications across Central Government, Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs), State Commissions, and Top Corporate MNC drives.

What We Provide

  • Verified Job Notifications: Opening dates, last date countdowns, age eligibility, and official PDF links.
  • Illustrated 100-Word Daily News: Curated morning (8 AM) and evening (8 PM) bulletins in Hindi and English.
  • All-India Boards & Degree Hub: Class 10th and 12th model papers across 28+ State Boards plus university degree question banks.
  • Teaching & Junior Engineer Hub: CTET, State TETs, SSC JE, and RRB JE branch-wise preparation.
  • TCS iON CBT Mock Test Simulator: Authentic computer-based exam practice with comprehensive post-test analytics and step-by-step solutions.

Editorial Guidelines & Source Attribution

All news items and job summaries published on RozgarSetu are synthesized from official government gazettes, Press Information Bureau (PIB), Employment News, and authorized recruitment portals. We maintain strict factual accuracy and adhere to fair-use copyright compliance.
